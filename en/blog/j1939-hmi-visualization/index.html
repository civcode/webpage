J1939 data visualization in an HMI

A sample interface concept for turning CAN/J1939 messages into clear engine, temperature, and diagnostic displays.

HMICAN BUSJ1939

Making vehicle data useful

Heavy-duty vehicle systems often expose information through SAE J1939 over CAN. Raw frames are useful to engineers, but operators need meaningful values, clear units, current status, and an immediate indication when data is no longer valid.

A practical visualization concept

A prototype HMI could decode selected parameter group numbers (PGNs) and suspect parameter numbers (SPNs), map valid values to gauges, and show explicit disconnected, stale, or not-available states. Each signal should have defined scaling, units, and a documented update rate.

What to share in a real post

A production-focused article could include a Qt screen recording, sample trace files with sensitive identifiers removed, a signal mapping table, and notes on testing against both recorded traffic and simulated bus errors.
