AI / HARDWARE

A 64 GB VRAM AI workstation

An illustrative look at designing a local AI workstation: GPU memory planning, cooling, Linux tooling, and repeatable model experiments.

AIGPULINUX

Illustrative sample content and diagrams — not completed project claims.

The idea

A local workstation can be useful when models, training data, or experiments should stay on hardware under your control. In this example project, the design target is 64 GB of total GPU VRAM, with an emphasis on predictable thermals and maintainable software.

Engineering decisions

A sensible plan starts with model sizes, memory bandwidth, PCIe lane allocation, power delivery, cooling, and storage throughput. A machine with several GPUs can have 64 GB of aggregate VRAM, but their memory is not automatically one shared 64 GB pool; splitting a workload requires compatible software and an appropriate parallelization strategy.

Possible next steps

The project note could document the actual hardware list, internal photographs, measured power draw, inference throughput, temperature readings, and lessons learned after the workstation is built.
